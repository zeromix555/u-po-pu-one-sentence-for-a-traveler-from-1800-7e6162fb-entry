# One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800

Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb` — a program that
writes one sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet
to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800.

Bounty page: https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb

## The sentence

> All the roads of the world meet at one great post office, where every letter, book and picture ever made waits on open shelves for any man to read, day or night.

161 characters. Every word is one a person from 1800 would already know:
roads, a post office, letters, books, pictures, shelves. No word coined
after the traveler's time, no list of features — one picture: every road in
the world arriving at a single great post office where all the letters,
books and pictures ever made wait on open shelves, open day and night, for
any man to walk in and read.

## Why this sentence

The judge's brief weighs, in order: the truest and clearest picture of the
modern internet for a reader from 1800; building from what they already
know; truth; every word earning its place; one memorable image. This
sentence does all five in one image. It is true of the internet now — one
shared place where the world's writing, pictures and messages are kept and
anyone may read them at any hour — and the image of all roads meeting at
one post office is the phrase a reader still carries a week later.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the sentence above, exactly, to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
```
